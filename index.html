01 About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Division of Robotics, Perception and Learning (RPL), working on Brain–Robot Interfaces, Multimodal Human–Robot Interaction, and Intelligent Voice Interfaces. My research bridges speech signal processing, affective computing, and human-centered AI, with a recent focus on emotion-aware voice interfaces for dementia and elderly care. I hold a Ph.D. in Medieninformatik from LMU Munich, and my work has been recognized with Best Paper Awards at CHI 2026 and IUI 2026.

Research Projects

2014 – 2015

Wearable Autism Voice Monitoring System

A wearable system to assess infantile autism by recording and analyzing speech features — fundamental, rhythm, and social-perceptual — to help detect stress in individuals with autistic disorder.

2015 – 2016

Wearable Autism Behavior Monitoring System

Analyzed behavioral differences between individuals with infantile autism and the general population to assess mental stress through speech and motion behavioral features.
